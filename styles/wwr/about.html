History

We accomplish the vision of Emaneke who has lived in California for above 24 years. When he came for holiday in Tana, he was surprised by the lack of leisure in water and the rush everyone has to go to the sea-beach. With the nearest beach being at seven hours drive distance, he decided that it was big time to make his fellow Tananarivian benefit from his experience from California. Thus started White Water Raft Tsiadana in 2013, with only five kayaks, twenty padels, and a joyful company. Over the years, the fame has spread and the team became more staffed. Safety-first is our obsession while allowing everyone, as well as foreign tourist, to discover Mada in a different way, remain safe, and have the adrenaline of their lives.
